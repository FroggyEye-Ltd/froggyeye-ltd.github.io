How it works

Get ready once,
know what to do later.

There is nothing to sign up for. Everything is stored on your phone, so the guides and tools still work when the power, the network or the signal does not.

01

Prepare

Work through the guide checklists in priority order and watch your readiness score go up. The app suggests the next few things to do, such as storing water or finding the stopcock.

02

Make a household plan

Write down where to meet, an out-of-area contact, who needs help, and where the stopcock, fuse box and gas meter are. Copy the whole plan as text in one tap to share it.

03

Respond

When something goes wrong, pick what is failing (power, water, signal, heating and more) and get one clear plan: right now, the first hour, today and the days after. In an emergency, call 999 first.

Why it works

Practical help that
works with no internet.

22 offline guides

Power cut, flooding, severe storm, cold weather, heatwave, gas leak and carbon monoxide and more. Each has a checklist in priority order, supplies to keep, common mistakes, regional notes, useful numbers and the public sources it is based on.

Household plan

Where to meet, an out-of-area contact, who needs help, and where the stopcock, fuse box and gas meter are. A readiness score shows what to do next, and a water storage calculator works out how much to keep.

One plan when it happens

Respond turns what is failing into one plan by time: right now, the first hour, today and the days after. Calling 999, the right-now steps and every safety step are free for any situation.

Torch and SOS light

A torch, and an SOS light signal in Morse code using the torch or the screen. An emergency card puts your numbers and household plan on one screen, and battery tips help your phone last longer.

Phone-to-phone chat

Send short messages over Bluetooth with no internet or mobile signal, through nearby phones that have the app. It needs other people with the app within range, and delivery is never guaranteed. Call 999 whenever you can.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your checklists, household plan and messages are kept on your phone. Private group messages are end-to-end encrypted; the public Nearby and SOS channels are not.

FAQ

The basics.

Is it available outside the UK?

Not yet. Blackout Survival Kit is made for UK households: the guides follow UK public guidance and use UK numbers such as 999, 105 for a power cut in Great Britain, and 111. Version 1.0 is available on the App Store and Google Play in the UK only.

Does it work with no internet?

Yes. The guides, checklists, household plan and tools are stored on your phone and work with no connection. You only need to be online to buy Pro; once unlocked, Pro works offline too.

Is this an official emergency or alerting app?

No. It does not receive government or emergency alerts and is not connected to the emergency services. In an emergency, call 999 (or 112).

What is free?

All of Prepare (the 22 guides, checklists, readiness score, household plan and water calculator) and all of Tools (torch, SOS light signal, emergency card, battery tips). In Respond, calling 999, the right-now steps and every safety step are free for any situation. In mesh chat, turning it on, passing on messages for others, reading every channel, sending on the public Nearby channel, joining a group, and sending an SOS or an "I am safe" message are free.

What does Pro add, and is it a subscription?

Pro is a one-time unlock, not a subscription. It adds the rest of the Respond plan (the first hour, today and the days after), the fridge and freezer food safety clock, the water ration calculator, and creating private groups and sending messages in them. The app shows the price for your storefront before you buy.

How does mesh chat work?

It sends short messages phone to phone over Bluetooth, through nearby phones that have the app, with no internet or mobile signal. Mesh chat and SOS pass messages phone to phone over Bluetooth, through nearby phones that have the app. They need other people with the app within range, and delivery is never guaranteed. Someone nearby who deliberately floods the mesh can delay an SOS by an hour or more, or keep it local. Call 999 whenever you can. Keep the app open for the best range.

Are my messages private?

Messages in private groups are end-to-end encrypted. The Nearby and SOS channels are public: anyone nearby with the app can read them. Messages you send go to nearby phones, and phones that pass them on keep copies for up to 6 hours. You can long-press any message to hide it, delete it or block its sender.

Can I report a message or a sender?

Yes. Long-press a message and choose Report. This blocks the sender on your phone and opens an email to info@froggyeye.com with the message, the sender's name and when it was sent; you choose whether to send it. Mesh chat has no server, so messages cannot be deleted from other phones. If you are threatened or in danger, call 999.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone. There is no account and no server of ours in the middle. The app contacts the App Store or Google Play only to show the price and check your purchase. The privacy policy has the full detail.

Does my family need to buy Pro separately?

On iPhone and iPad, people in your Family Sharing group can use Pro too. On Android, Pro is tied to the Google account that bought it. Reading a group is free for everyone; creating groups and sending in them is Pro.

I bought Pro. How do I get it back on a new phone?

Open Pro in the app's Settings and restore your purchase there, signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with.

How do I get help?

Email info@froggyeye.com and tell us which phone you use and what happened. A person will reply.
