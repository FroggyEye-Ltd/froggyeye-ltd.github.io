Welcome to PostPilot.

Your license is on its way to your email — usually within 1-2 minutes. Here's how to get up and running:

1

Check your email for the license key

Polar (our payment processor) sends a receipt + your PostPilot license key to the email you checked out with. The key looks like a long UUID string.

Can't find it? Check spam, or open the Polar customer portal.

2

Download & install PostPilot

If you haven't already:

⌘ macOS download ⊞ Windows download

See the first-launch help if you get a security warning on first open.

3

Activate your license in the app

Open PostPilot → click Settings in the sidebar → License tab → paste your key → click Activate.

The green "active" pill confirms you're set. The Compose, Warm-up and Custom tabs all unlock immediately.

4

Bring your own Anthropic API key

PostPilot uses Claude under the hood — you provide your own Anthropic API key. Sign up free at console.anthropic.com, then paste into Settings → Setup.

Typical usage runs $1–5/month on top of your license.
