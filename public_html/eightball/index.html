FAQ

The basics.

Is this just the Magic 8 Ball app?

It's the Magic 8 Ball app done properly. Real physics, beautiful art, no ad-stuffing. The version we wanted to exist.

Why pay for a Magic 8 Ball?

You don't have to — the free version is genuinely complete. Plus exists if you want all the themes and you'd rather pay once than see ads.

Does it actually predict the future?

It actually does not. Probability says it'll be right slightly under 50% of the time. The fun is in the ritual, not the result.

Can my kids use it?

Yes — no inappropriate answers, no scary themes, rated 4+/Everyone. Bedtime questions encouraged.

Does it work offline?

Always. Shake works on a plane, in a tent, on the moon.

Never. Eight Ball Wisdom doesn't push notifications. The ball waits for you.
