Checking a car yourself?

Cellgrade reads a car's batteries through an OBD adapter and grades each one from A to E, with the three measurements behind the grade. That much is free, on both phones.

iPhone Android

Have only the code?

The code is the report's own fingerprint. It proves a report has not been edited when you hold both, and on its own it is sixteen characters anyone can type. Scan the QR code on the document and the whole report is checked at once.

A Cellgrade Battery Report is a record of a measurement. It is not an inspection, a warranty, a guarantee of future performance, or a valuation, and it is not endorsed by or connected to any vehicle manufacturer.
