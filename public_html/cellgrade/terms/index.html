1. What Cellgrade is

1.1Cellgrade is a mobile application that connects to a vehicle through a third-party OBD adapter, reads data reported by the vehicle's own electronic modules, and presents that data as readings, insights, alerts, a letter grade from A to E, and optionally a battery report ("certificate").

1.2Every figure Cellgrade shows originates from the vehicle's battery management system, other vehicle modules, or the adapter's own voltage measurement. Cellgrade does not measure a battery directly and does not verify what the vehicle reports.

1.3A grade is Cellgrade's interpretation of that reported data using published thresholds. It is a summary of a measurement taken at one moment, not a statement about the physical condition, safety, value, remaining life or future performance of any battery or vehicle.

2. What Cellgrade is not

2.1Cellgrade is not an inspection. It cannot see physical condition, corrosion, wiring, terminals, mountings, leaks, damage, or anything outside what the vehicle's modules report.

2.2Cellgrade is not a warranty or guarantee. Nothing in the app, a grade or a certificate promises that a battery or vehicle will perform in any way, for any period, or at all.

2.3Cellgrade is not a valuation and is not professional, mechanical, financial or legal advice. It does not replace a test by a qualified person with the vehicle in front of them.

2.4Cellgrade is not endorsed by, connected to or approved by any vehicle or battery manufacturer. Vehicle and product names are used only to identify the vehicles the app can read.

Where the app marks a map or a reading as unconfirmed, derived, community, not readable or not evaluated, that label is part of the reading and you should not rely on the figure.

The signature shows only that the report has not been altered since it was issued. It does not show that the data is correct, complete or representative.

4.2A certificate says nothing about the vehicle at any other time, including at the time anyone reads it.
