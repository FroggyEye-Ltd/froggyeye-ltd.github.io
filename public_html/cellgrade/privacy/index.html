Cellgrade Privacy Policy

How Cellgrade handles data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and what the app reads from your car stays on your phone unless you choose to send it.

Effective date: 22 September 2026
Controller: Froggy Eye Ltd, England and Wales · info@froggyeye.com

Last updated: 22 September 2026

In plain words. Cellgrade has no account, no advertising and no analytics. What it reads from your car stays on your phone unless you choose to send a scan or share a report. We never see your car's identification number unless you choose to include it in a scan.

1. What the app stores on your phone

The app keeps, on your device only: readings from your vehicle, the history of those readings, grades, insights, alerts, any certificates you have bought, your settings, and your purchase entitlements. You can delete readings and history in the app, and deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

2. What leaves your phone, and when

Nothing leaves your phone unless one of the following happens, each of which you start yourself.

2.1 Buying Pro or a certificate. The purchase is made through Apple's App Store or Google Play under their privacy terms. We receive from the store only a proof that a purchase was made. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

2.2 Signing a certificate. To sign a report, the app sends to our signing service (run on Cloudflare) a cryptographic digest of the report, the time, a plausibility summary (a handful of yes/no flags about the reading), and the store's proof of purchase. The service does not receive the vehicle identification number, the readings, or anything that identifies you. It records the digest and the purchase identifier so that each credit signs one report, and it returns a signature.

2.3 Sharing a report. The certificate's QR code and PDF contain the whole report, including any vehicle identification number you chose to include. Sharing them is your choice and is not a transfer to us. Anyone who scans the QR code verifies it in their own browser; the verification page sends nothing to us.

2.4 Sending a scan. If you use Add my car or Report a problem, the app shows you exactly what will be sent before you send it, field by field, and that list is generated from the file rather than written beside it. By default the vehicle identification number is masked to its first eleven characters (make, model, plant and model year). You can change what is sent.

The file includes what kind of adapter you used — its make and the firmware version string it reports, such as "ELM327 v2.2" — because a reading can only be judged against the hardware that took it. It does not include any identifier for your adapter or your phone: no Bluetooth address, no device name you chose, no install identifier, no advertising identifier. It does not include your odometer reading, your location, or anything from any other app.

A scan can only be sent from a real adapter reading a real car. A reading produced by the app's built-in demonstration adapter cannot be sent at all.

What you send is stored by us to build and improve vehicle data maps, and may be published in a data map under an open licence in a form that does not identify you. You can see your contributions and withdraw them in Settings.

2.5 Support email. If you email us, we hold that email to answer it.

3. What we do not do

We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers, tracking, fingerprinting, or third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not sell data. We do not link any scan to a person.

4. Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to talk to the OBD adapter. Used only while the app is open.
  • Camera: only when you choose to scan a certificate's QR code. No images are stored.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders or battery alerts. They are generated on the phone.

5. Legal basis and retention

Contributed scans and signing records are processed on the basis of your consent (you start each one) and our legitimate interest in maintaining accurate vehicle data maps and preventing certificate fraud. Signing records are kept for as long as certificates need to be verifiable. Support emails are kept for two years.

Contributed scans are kept until you withdraw them, except data already incorporated into a published map. Withdrawing deletes the scan itself — the readings, the adapter conversation and your typed answers — and it does not delete the receipt we signed for it. We keep that receipt, marked withdrawn, and it holds the first eleven characters of the identification number, which car model the scan was for, and a record that a free Cellgrade Pro grant was issued. We keep it for three reasons: the receipt is the only thing that proves a withdrawal was authorised, since we hold no account; a scan you send again is then recognised as the same scan rather than counted twice; and the reward you earned is not taken back when you take the data back. None of it identifies you, and we do not keep the address you sent from — where we have to count how many places a grant has been handed to, we keep a shortened one-way hash of the address and never the address itself.

6. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what we hold, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to processing, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because we hold no account and cannot link a scan to you, the app's own Withdraw control is usually the only way to remove a contribution. Contact info@froggyeye.com for anything else.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date shows the current version.
