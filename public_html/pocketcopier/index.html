How it works

Snap the page, check the copy,
share the PDF.

PocketCopier uses the document camera built into your phone to find the edges of the page, then does the part a scanner app usually leaves to you: it squares the page up, removes the shadows and turns grey paper white.

01

Snap it, or import it

Point the camera at the page, or pick a photo you already took from your gallery. Scan several pages in one go, or add more later. An angled shot on a busy desk is fine.

02

Auto-align does the straightening

The page is found, squared up and cleaned: shadows removed, paper turned white, text kept crisp. When the edges are unclear the page is flagged for you to check rather than cropped wrongly, and Adjust corners lets you drag each corner into place yourself.

03

Export as PDF or PNG

Reorder, rotate, retake or delete pages, then export one multi-page PDF on A4 or US Letter, or fit the sheet to the page. Or save each page as a PNG to your gallery. A copy leaves through your phone's share menu, when you decide.

FAQ

The basics.

Is it a scanner app?

It works as a document scanner and a PDF scanner, but what you get back is a proper photocopy: the page squared up, the shadows gone, grey paper turned white. If you only want the photo tidied, the Original and Grayscale filters do that.

What if the page is shot at an angle?

That is the normal case. Auto-align finds the edges of the page and straightens it, even on a busy desk. Every page keeps a Before view, so you can see the original photo next to the copy.

What if Auto-align gets the edges wrong?

When the edges are unclear the page is flagged for you to check rather than cropped wrongly. Open Adjust corners, drag each corner into place and apply. The original photo is kept, so nothing is lost.

Can I scan more than one page into a PDF?

Yes. Scan several pages in one go or add more later, reorder, rotate, retake or delete them, then export one PDF on A4 or US Letter, or fit the sheet to the page. Free documents hold up to 5 pages; Pro removes the limit.

Can I use a photo I already took?

Yes. Import it from your gallery and PocketCopier straightens and cleans it the same way as a fresh capture.

Where do my documents go?

They are kept in the app on your phone, and pages are processed on your phone. A copy goes anywhere else only when you share it through your phone's share menu or save a PNG to your gallery. There is no account and no sign-in. On Android, the Google document-scanner component built into the app sends Google limited diagnostic information, never your documents; the privacy policy explains this.

What does Free include, and what does Pro add?

Free: unlimited documents, PDF and PNG export, no watermark, up to 5 pages per document and 5 pages a day. Pro is a one-time £9.99 purchase that removes both page limits and adds Magic colour and ZIP export. No subscription.

What is Magic colour?

A Pro filter for pages where the colour matters. It turns the paper white while keeping the colours of the ink, so a highlighted worksheet or a form signed in blue comes out clean without going black and white.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is one payment. It restores on a new phone through Restore purchase in Settings, and the price shown in the app comes from your own storefront.
