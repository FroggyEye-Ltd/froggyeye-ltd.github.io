How it works

Pick. Save.
Fingers crossed.

Choosing your numbers is half the fun of playing. Lucky Numbers makes that half properly good — and leaves the ticket to you.

01

Land on a line

Lucky Dip, hot/cold stats, birthdays and dream symbols, a shake-the-phone ball machine, even a photo. Lock in numbers you love, exclude ones you don't.

02

Save it to My Slips

Name your lines, group them by game, keep them as long as you like. Optional reminders nudge you on draw days — Wednesday & Saturday for Lotto, Tuesday & Friday for EuroMillions.

03

Check the results

After the draw, type in the winning line and the offline checker marks every matched number across your saved slips, with an indicative prize tier.

FAQ

The straight answers.

Does Lucky Numbers sell lottery tickets?

No. It never sells tickets, takes wagers, or processes payments for play. It helps you choose and organise numbers — buying a ticket is entirely between you and the official operators.

Can the app improve my chances of winning?

No — and we say so everywhere. Lottery draws are random. No number choice, hot/cold statistic, or strategy changes the odds. The stats and pickers are entertainment, not an edge.

Is Lucky Numbers 18+?

Yes. National Lottery products are 18+ in the UK, so the app asks you to confirm your age on first launch and carries 18+ and BeGambleAware signposting throughout.

Does it work offline?

Yes — every feature runs on your device, including the results checker (type in the winning line and it checks your saved slips). The only network use is Apple's or Google's own purchase system if you buy Pro.

What happens to my photo in Lucky moment?

The photo's data is used once, in memory, to generate a line of numbers, then discarded. It is never saved by the app, uploaded, or shared — and we never see it.

What does Pro cost?

£4.99, once. No subscription. Pro unlocks planning and organisation tools — the Strategy Lab, unlimited saved slips, more lines at once and deeper stats. It never sells predictions or better odds, because those don't exist.

Where can I get help with gambling?

BeGambleAware.org offers free, confidential advice, and the GamCare National Gambling Helpline is 0808 8020 133 (24/7, free from UK phones).
