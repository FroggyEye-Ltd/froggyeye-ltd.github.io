How it works

Wait. React. Repeat.
The whole game in three beats.

No tutorials to sit through, no currencies to farm. Open it, wait for the signal, and find out how fast you actually are.

01

Wait for the event

Every level hides its trigger behind an unpredictable wait. You never know whether it lands in one second or ten — so you can't pre-tap your way through.

02

Tap at the exact moment

The window opens and closes. Early is a fail. Late is a fail. Later levels want several taps inside that same shrinking window.

03

Climb the ranks

Clear a level and the next one tightens the screws. Your best times post to a real-time global leaderboard so you can see where you sit.

Why bother

Everyone thinks they're quick.
Very few people are.

God Level strips the genre back to one honest question: when the signal comes, how long does it take you to move? No power-ups to buy your way past it, no difficulty slider to hide behind. Just you, a window, and a number.

The scenarios start deliberately mundane — a microwave ding, a barber calling your name, a boarding announcement — because that's where reaction time actually lives. Then the God tier throws the everyday out and drops you at the edge of a black hole with 200 milliseconds to react.

FAQ

The basics.

How does the game actually work?

Every level hides an event behind an unpredictable wait. When the event fires, you have a fixed window to tap. Tap before it fires and you fail; miss the window and you fail. Clear it and you move up.

Is it free?

Yes — the download is free and levels 1 to 30 are free to play, with ads. The ten God Levels are behind a single one-off in-app purchase, God Levels Unlock, at £1.99. There's no subscription.

What are the God Levels?

Levels 31 to 40 — a separate tier with reaction windows as tight as 200ms, running from the Quantum Realm to The Absolute, the final level in the game.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with Apple or Google, which is what syncs your progress across devices and puts your times on the global leaderboard. You can reset your progress or delete your account entirely from Settings.

What are multi-tap challenges?

In the later levels one tap isn't enough — the window asks for several taps in a row, up to five. Same shrinking window, more to do inside it.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Sound effects, background music, vibration and event notifications each have their own switch in Settings.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. God Level is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and it's rated 12+ for frequent or intense contests.

How fast are you, really?

Free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Forty levels between you and The Absolute. The path to becoming a god starts with a single tap.
